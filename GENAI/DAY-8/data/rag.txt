Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is an artificial intelligence architecture 
that optimizes large language model (LLM) responses by grounding them in external, 
authoritative knowledge bases.   
Standard LLMs rely solely on static training data, 
making them susceptible to factual errors and hallucinations. 
RAG addresses this through a three-step cycle:   
Retrieval: When a prompt is received, the system queries external document stores—typically indexed within vector databases—to locate the most contextually relevant information.   Augmentation: The retrieved excerpts are inserted directly into the prompt context alongside the original request.   Generation: The model synthesizes an up-to-date, grounded, and verifiable answer.   This eliminates the continuous cost of model retraining while providing accurate, domain-specific insights.
